# Infant cry reference

Cries are graded signals; acoustic structure correlates with urgency more
than with specific needs. Be humble — confidence on infants should usually
sit in the 0.4–0.7 band.

## Classes

- **Hunger / rhythmical** — repeated cry–breath–cry pattern, steady envelope,
  moderate pitch (350–450 Hz), flat or gently falling contour per unit.
- **Discomfort** — similar pitch but more irregular envelope, longer pauses,
  variable intensity.
- **Pain / urgent** — sudden onset, higher peak f0, tense (high spectral
  centroid), long flat units with little modulation. Flag as urgent.
- **Attention / grizzle** — low intensity, intermittent, short voiced units
  alternating with pauses. Not a full cry.
